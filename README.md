This repository contains the corpus data for the dissertation "Evaluating Speaker Stance Investment across Modalities" by Grace LeFevre Richmond (2026). For the theoretical background, research questions, methodological details, and analyses associated with these data, please refer to the dissertation document. 

Below is a brief description of the files contained in this repo and which studies they are associated with. 

Study 1:
- study1_transcripts.csv: Complete transcripts of SIIRC responses.
- study1_speaker_demographics.csv: Selected demographic and background information for SIIRC speakers.
- study1_item_results.csv: Speaker-reported investment and other item-level results from Study 1.
- study1_acoustic_features.csv: Acoustic features used in analyses, calculated for each corpus item.

Study 2:
- study2_perceiver_results.csv: Human perceiver judgments from Study 2, split by reader and listener.

Study 3:
- study3_LLM_audio_results.csv: LLM judgments made based on audio input.
- study3_LLM_text_results.csv: LLM judgments made based on text input.

The SIIRC audio recordings are maintained separately and are available upon request.
